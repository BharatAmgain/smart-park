About SmartPark

Revolutionizing urban parking with smart technology, real-time availability, and seamless payments.

Our Mission

To eliminate parking frustration by providing a real-time, intelligent parking solution that helps drivers find, reserve, and pay for parking instantly – saving time, reducing congestion, and lowering carbon emissions in Kathmandu Valley.

Our Vision

A future where urban mobility is seamless, parking is stress-free, and every parking spot is utilized efficiently through data-driven insights.

Real-time Maps

Live tracking of available slots with turn-by-turn directions.

Multiple Payments

eSewa, Khalti, and card payments – secure and instant.

Smart Analytics

Dashboard with occupancy trends and vehicle compatibility.

Why Choose SmartPark?

  • No more circling – find parking instantly
  • Reserve slots up to 24 hours in advance
  • Email OTP verification for secure bookings
  • Real-time slot status with color-coded markers
  • Detailed invoices and cancellation with refund
  • Optimized for all vehicle types: Sedan, SUV, EV, Bike, Truck
  • Turn-by-turn navigation to your reserved slot
  • SMS and Email reminders for active sessions

Our Coverage

SmartPark covers over 500+ parking zones across the entire Kathmandu Valley including:

  • Kathmandu Metropolitan
  • Lalitpur Metropolitan
  • Bhaktapur Municipality
  • Kirtipur Municipality
  • Tokha Municipality
  • Chandragiri Municipality
  • All major shopping malls
  • Major hospitals
  • Educational institutions
